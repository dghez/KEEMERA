Bestiary · The first shard

The Keemera of the Thorned Gate

Bestia alata, ungue et squama.

Eagle in the head, bat in the wing, lion in the haunch, and serpent from the waist down. The scribes could never agree which beast it was, so they drew all of them at once.

It keeps a single rose for every gate it has guarded, and a flame that is said to tie itself in knots when lied to.

Found
Gates, ruins, margins of old books
Temper
Watchful, slow to forgive
Weakness
A true word, plainly spoken
Bestiary · The second shard

The Gorgon of the Salt Shore

Femina serpentina, oculo lapidea.

Half woman, half sea-serpent, crowned with a nest of living snakes. She sits among the wrecks she made, a broken lyre at her tail, and the tide brings her the rest.

Sailors say her gaze turns flesh to stone, so they learned to watch her only in the water, or in the polish of a shield.

Found
Rocky shores, the wake of shipwrecks
Temper
Proud, patient, never alone
Weakness
Her own reflection
